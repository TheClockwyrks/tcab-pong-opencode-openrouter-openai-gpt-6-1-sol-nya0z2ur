# Carom

Two paddles. One ball. A court that never gives you the same angle twice. Carom is
a quick-fire paddle duel for a solo challenge against the machine or a local
grudge match with a friend. The first serve gives you a breath to get in position.
Then the ricochets begin.

A clean center hit drives the ball straight across. Catch it near the end of your
paddle and send it wide. Slide as you strike, and your return takes on spin: a
gentle bend that can grow into a wicked curve. Watch the trail, trust your timing,
and turn a desperate save into a shot your opponent never saw coming.

The two amber obstacles are always swaying and turning. Their tilted faces open
up new bank shots and close familiar lanes. Play through the gap, skim a corner,
or bounce a curve into the other half of the court. Every paddle contact speeds
up the rally, so the next decision arrives a little sooner.

First to eleven takes the match, but you need a two-point lead. At deuce, every
return matters. Find one last opening, hold your nerve on match point, and send
the ball beyond the other paddle. Then play again: there is always another angle.
